# Flipkart iPhone Sales Analysis — Python

## Business Objective
Analyze iPhone listings to understand pricing, discounts, ratings, review volume, and customer engagement across models and configurations.

## Dataset
**62 rows × 11 columns**, including product, brand, sale price, MRP, discount, rating, ratings count, reviews, and RAM.

## Key Findings
- Top-rated models are concentrated around the 4.5+ rating range.
- iPhone 8 Plus shows very high review/rating engagement.
- The analysis indicates stronger engagement among lower-priced/older models.
- High discounts do not necessarily correspond to higher ratings.
- The analyzed price range is approximately **₹29,999–₹1,40,900**.

## Analysis Workflow
**Cleaning → price/discount analysis → rating analysis → engagement comparison → visualization**

## Tech Stack
**Python | Pandas | Plotly | EDA | Retail/Product Analytics**
