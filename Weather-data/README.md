# Weather Data Analysis — Python

## Business Objective
Analyze hourly weather observations to understand temperature, humidity, visibility, wind, pressure, and weather-condition patterns.

## Dataset
**8,784 hourly records from 2012** with timestamp, temperature, dew point, humidity, wind speed, visibility, pressure, and weather condition.

## Analysis Performed
The notebook answers 15 analytical questions covering:
- Wind-speed distribution
- Clear/snow/fog observations
- Missing-value validation
- Mean visibility
- Pressure variability
- Humidity variance
- Weather-condition statistics
- Conditional filtering

## Reported Findings
- **1,326** clear-weather records
- **474** records with wind speed of 4 km/h
- **27.66 km** mean visibility
- **0.844 kPa** pressure standard deviation
- **390** snow records
- **150** fog records

## Tech Stack
**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | EDA**
