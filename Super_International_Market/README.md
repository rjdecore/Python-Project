# International Sales & Profit Analysis — Python

## Business Objective
Analyze international sales data to understand revenue and profit trends, regional performance, market contribution, product demand, and profitability.

## Business Questions
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which regions and markets contribute most?
- Which cities and states are most profitable?
- Which products have the highest quantity sold?
- How is sales/profit distributed across markets?

## Analysis Workflow
- Data cleaning and consistency checks
- Date preparation
- Sales/profit trend analysis
- Category and sub-category comparison
- Regional and market analysis
- Product ranking
- Business visualization

## Important Data Treatment
The existing analysis documents handling of negative profit values during preparation; this assumption should be discussed when interpreting the results.

## Tech Stack
**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Sales Analytics**

## Key Findings & Business Value
The analysis compares sales and profit over time, region/market contribution, profitable locations, product quantities and market-level distributions. Use notebook outputs for exact best/worst markets and products. Document the treatment of negative-profit values because it changes the interpretation of profitability.

Business teams can use the results to identify markets and products for deeper profitability review and to distinguish revenue growth from profit performance.
